Computer vision is the field of teaching machines to understand images and
video.
A document scanner is a small example of this idea. It finds the page ina
photo,
straightens the view, and removes shadows so that the text is easy to read.
First, the program converts the photo to lay and finds the strong edges.
Next, it looks for the largest shape with four corners, which is usually the page.
Then it stretches that shape into a flat rectangle. Finally, it cleans the lighting
So that the writing looks dark and the paper looks white.
